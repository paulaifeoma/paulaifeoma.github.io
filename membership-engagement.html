CRM Membership Engagement

Turning membership data into insights that support better segmentation, targeted engagement, and informed decision-making.

Project Overview

This project explored membership and CRM data to understand engagement patterns, assess data quality, examine segmentation and event participation, and identify opportunities for more targeted member communication.

Analytical Approach

The analysis focused on identifying engagement patterns, segmenting members based on their activity and using the insights to support more targeted communication and engagement strategies.

I prepared and analysed the CRM dataset through a structured data analytics process:

  • Reviewed the dataset to understand member attributes, engagement measures and available activity data.
  • Cleaned and validated member records to improve data quality and consistency.
  • Analysed participation and response patterns to identify areas of stronger and weaker engagement.
  • Developed calculated measures and KPIs in Power BI to support interactive analysis.
  • Designed a three-page dashboard to move from an overall view of member engagement to more detailed analysis of engagement activities.
  • Interpreted the findings to identify practical opportunities for targeted communication and member engagement.

    • Key Analysis

    The analysis focused on five key areas:

  • Member Engagement: Examined overall levels of member activity and participation to understand how actively members interact with the organisation.
  • Event Participation: Reviewed event attendance and participation patterns to understand which member groups were more actively involved in events.
  • Analysed event attendance by organisation and occupation
  • Analysed event participation across different event types to identify patterns in member preferences and engagement.
  • Analysed member engagement and retention: Examined renewal behaviour alongside engagement activity to identify relationships between member participation and ongoing membership.

    • Dashboard

    The visualisations below present the key findings from the analysis.

    Membership CRM Dashboard

    This first dashboard provides a high-level view of overall CRM member engagement. It brings together key membership and engagement KPIs, allowing users to quickly understand the size of the member base and the level of interaction across different activities.
    The dashboard includes summary metrics and visualisations covering areas such as member activity, event registration and participation, members' demography and events summary.

    Purpose: To provide management with a quick overview of the current engagement picture and identify areas requiring further investigation.


    Event Attendance and Participation

    This dashboard provides a deeper analysis of how members interact with the organisation. It examines event attendance across different event types, organisations, ocupation, allowing patterns to be compared rather than relying only on overall averages.
    The dashboard focuses on identifying highly engaged and less-engaged groups, understanding the types of events generating participation, and examining variations in engagement behaviour.
    This analysis helps move from simply measuring engagement to understanding where engagement is occurring and which member groups may require different approaches.

    Purpose: To support more targeted member engagement by identifying patterns across member groups and engagement activities.

    Member Engagement Dashboard

    This dashboard focuses on the relationship between member engagement activities and ongoing membership behaviour.
    It analyses membership status, engagement segments, engagement trend, and engagement across differnt organisations.
    This level of analysis is to identify patterns that may help inform future engagement strategies. The dashboard allows users to compare engagement activity and investigate whether particular member groups demonstrate different participation patterns.

    Purpose: To provide insights that can support member relationship management, member segmentation, and member retention initiatives.

    Key Insights

    The analysis highlighted several areas that can inform member engagement decisions:

  • Member engagement varies across different activities, indicating that a single engagement strategy may not be appropriate for all members.
  • Event participation is a good indicator of member engagement and can help identify groups with stronger or weaker involvement.

    • Management Recommendations

    • Strengthen CRM data quality: Continue regular validation and maintenance of member records to improve the completeness, consistency and reliability of CRM data.
    • Develop targeted member segments: Tailor communication and engagement based on characteristic and behaviour of each segment. Use relevant member attributes such as highly engaged, At-risk, dormant to target events and communications.
    • Use events as an engagement channel to increase participation and to encourage the less-active members.
    • Establish ongoing CRM reporting: The dasboard should be used as a repeatable reporting tool so that engagemnet trends can be monitored over time.

    Tools Used

  • Microsoft Power BI: data modelling, DAX measures, interactive dashboards and visualisation
  • Microsoft Excel: data preparation, validation and exploratory analysis
  • Power Query: data cleaning and transformation

    • Skills

    Data Cleaning, Exploratory Data Analysis, Data modelling, DAX, KPI Development, Data Visualisation, Dashboard Design and Business Insight Generation, Business Analysis, Business Intelligence.

    Project Outcome

    The project demonstrates how CRM data can be transformed into an interactive business intelligence solution that supports understanding of member behaviour and engagement.
    Rather than focusing only on reporting activity levels, the analysis connects member data, engagement behaviour and business questions to provide a more structured basis for targeted communication, campaign planning, event engagement and retention analysis.
    The three-page Power BI dashboard provides management with both a high-level overview and the ability to drill into specific engagement areas, supporting a more informed and data-driven approach to CRM management.
